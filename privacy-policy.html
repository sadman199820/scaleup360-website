Privacy policy

How The ScaleUp 360 handles enquiries and information used by this website.

When you request a quote

The form sends your name, email, selected services, portfolio reference and project brief through Web3Forms so our team can respond and discuss your project. Emailing us directly also shares your address and message with our inbox. Please keep passwords and customer records out of your brief.

Site operation

Cloudflare delivers this site and provides traffic and performance measurement. The theme switch saves your preference in your browser. Fonts, video thumbnails and embedded players can make requests to Google or YouTube. Read the Web3Forms privacy policy for information about its processing.

Questions about your information

Email info@thescaleup360.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of an enquiry. Include the address you used to contact us.
